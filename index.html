How It Works

📊

Monte Carlo Engine

50,000+ independent simulations sampling EBITDA paths and exit multiples to generate distribution of recovery outcomes across the capital structure.

🔄

PIK Accretion

Models payment-in-kind interest accruals on applicable instruments, compounding at specified rates to calculate true principal due at exit.

⚖️

Ch.11 Costs

Deducts professional fees (legal, accounting, restructuring advisors) from enterprise value before waterfall allocation to creditors and equity.

🎯

Fulcrum Analysis

Identifies the security most likely to recover 40-80% (the fulcrum) based on simulation results, indicating most risk-reward concentrated tranche.

💰

Market-Implied EBITDA

Reverse-engineers from trading prices and discount rate to infer what market thinks enterprise value and EBITDA recovery look like.

📈

Waterfall Allocation

Applies strict intercreditor priority, covenant restrictions, and use-of-proceeds requirements to distribute exit value per actual terms.
